# A9b mechanism reachability

## Verdict

The exact 298 K production initialization contains **320** nonfrozen,
original-lattice cation targets. Live Petra transition replay proves a legal
release path for **260** targets and a topology-level NO-GO for **60** boundary
targets:

| kind | targets | reachable | topology NO-GO |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Al | 160 | 140 | 20 |
| Si | 160 | 120 | 40 |
| **total** | **320** | **260** | **60** |

The 60 NO-GO targets repeat once per x-period at template offsets 3, 4, and 7.
Their exact structural caps are:

- `Al.l0` at offset 3 can remove only four nonfrozen intact adjacent bridges;
  two additional bridges are frozen, so it stops at `Al.l4`, below `Al.l6`.
- `Si.oh0` at offset 4 has only two nonfrozen intact adjacent bridges and stops
  at `Si.oh2`, below `Si.oh4`.
- `Si.oh1` at offset 7 has no nonfrozen intact adjacent bridge and remains at
  `Si.oh1`, below `Si.oh4`.

Reverse condensation restores the same bridge increment it removes. Adsorption
can create hydrolyzed witnesses, but converting an initially vacant oxygen into
an intact bridge requires the compensating condensation decrement; neither
operation raises these structural upper bounds. Initial absence of a
`@hydrolyzed` witness is therefore **not** used as a NO-GO claim.

## Auditable path proof


The generator validates the zero-step PGIF against a fresh live deck

## Rare-event method

The selected route is finite-horizon reaction-tilted CTMC importance sampling:

- `BiasedCtmc` multiplies selected nonzero physical reaction propensities by
  finite positive factors. It never seeds, rekinds, or relabels a lattice site.
- For physical rates `lambda_j` and biased rates
  `lambda'_j = factor_j * lambda_j`, each fired path segment updates
  `log(P/Q)` by
  `(sum(lambda') - sum(lambda)) * dt - ln(factor_fired)`. A deadline-censored
  segment omits the fired-event term.
- The estimator for preregistered horizon `T` and replica count `N` is
  `mean(exp(log(P/Q)) * N_original_lattice_release(T)) / T`.
- Original-lattice membership is frozen from the zero-step state. Desorption of
  a cation adsorbed onto an initially empty site is never counted.
- Every replica stops at `T` or at the fixed one-million-event safety cap.
  Nonfinite/underflowed/overflowed weights or an effective-sample-size fraction
  below 0.1 invalidate the scenario.
- Zero lineage-valid observations produce exactly zero, remain censored, and
  are never eligible for an ordinal sensitivity rank.

The executable bounded runner is:

```bash
cargo run -p petra-deck --example a9b_importance_sampling -- DECK OUT [N] [T]
```

A smoke run with `N=2`, `T=1e-12` completed as
`censored-zero-observation`, emitted estimate `0.0`, ESS `2.0`, and
`rank_eligible=false`.

## Verification

An independent verifier reproduced the deck/snapshot/report hashes, the corrected
260/60 census, the 1,020-event replay, the site-0 path, all three boundary caps,
and reverse/adsorption stress behavior. Across two cold-review cycles it exposed
five false-green paths: initial-local rather than live-graph reachability, an
invalid-kind witness-removal adversary, RNG consumption after failed biased
transitions, site-label lineage that double-counted a re-adsorbed reservoir ion,
and per-replica weight underflow hidden by aggregate ESS. The implementation now
uses live Petra replay, kind-valid topology reasoning, complete failed-transition
RNG/state/weight atomicity, a consumable original-ion ledger, and per-weight
normal-finite validation before aggregation.

Regression coverage includes:

- full live forward closure with exact 260/60 census and representative boundary
  caps;
- release lineage for reachable Al and Si targets;
- reservoir-origin exclusion;
- production-deck biased-CTMC execution and zero-observation behavior;
- exact likelihood arithmetic, deadline weighting, unit-bias parity, invalid
  bias rejection, and complete failed-transition atomicity.

This card supplies an executable sampling route, but the 60 topology NO-GOs mean
that a future sensitivity campaign must either exclude those centers by a
preregistered estimand or change the fixed-boundary model. It must not silently
interpret them as sampled zeros.
