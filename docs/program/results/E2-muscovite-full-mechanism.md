# E2 muscovite full mechanism — scheduled species/defect demonstration

**Card:** `E2-muscovite-full-mechanism`
**Scope:** phase-2 mechanism demonstration and first comparison, not a kinetic
fit and not a grain-scale simulation

## Verdict

**PASS as a deterministic mechanism demonstration.** The schema-v2 deck starts
from E1's paired-OH/divacancy model and adds all phase-2 state variables:
extended-defect zones, explicit bonded/delaminated interfaces, separate
⁴⁰Ar/³⁹Ar/³⁶Ar populations, recoil-initialized octahedral ³⁹Ar traps, and a
500–700 °C incremental-heating schedule. Two exact-seed runs produced
byte-identical `populations.csv` and `observables.csv` across all five schedule
boundaries.

This is deliberately **not** a fitted reconstruction of Sletten & Onstott
(1998). The delamination, extended-zone, and trap-escape barriers are sensitivity
proxies pending the separate phase-3 barrier campaign. They are labelled as
such in the deck and products; no proxy is presented as computed kinetics.

## Mechanism and provenance

- `petra/decks/muscovite-full-mechanism.toml` is generated byte-for-byte by
  `petra/scripts/build_muscovite_full_deck.py`; the test suite rejects drift.
- The 66 kcal/mol pristine-gallery divacancy hop is the Nteme et al. (2022)
  published anchor already used by E1.
- Extended zones are explicit `Defect_zone.extended` states. Their 40 kcal/mol
  hop is the Nteme et al. (2023) six-order enhancement mapped to a phase-2
  barrier proxy, not a computed defect-zone barrier.
- Dehydroxylation keeps E1's 57/60/69 kcal/mol published-envelope table.
- Delamination is an explicit `Interface.bonded → delaminated` local transition.
  It can fire only next to an `OH_acceptor.O_residual`; there is no global rate
  multiplier. Its nominal 58 kcal/mol barrier is bracketed by ±5 kcal/mol.
- Recoil-initialized ³⁹Ar occupies explicit `Octahedral_trap.Ar39_trapped`
  states and must escape through a guarded local transition before gallery
  release. The 64 kcal/mol escape value is also a labelled proxy.
- Every gallery hop and release rule names the isotope (`Ar40`, `Ar39`, or
  `Ar36`) and the pristine/extended or surface/delamination pathway. Scheduled
  CLI output reports each isotope state separately; boundary inventory loss is
  therefore an exact species-release count because hops and trap escape conserve
  isotope inventories.

The compact representative volume is `4 × 4 × 6` unit cells (768 Petra sites),
not a 52–64 µm flake or 3000 µm disk. Seed 1998 initialized 12 ⁴⁰Ar, 38 ³⁹Ar
(including 34 octahedral traps), and 1 ³⁶Ar. Those small counts are adequate for
a deterministic wiring/spectrum demonstration but not uncertainty estimates.

## Incremental-heating result

The five 600 s segments are 500, 550, 600, 650, and 700 °C. The run completed
all exact wall-time boundaries in 277 events:

| Step | °C | Δ⁴⁰Ar | Δ³⁹Ar | Δ³⁶Ar | cumulative ⁴⁰/³⁹/³⁶ | synthetic age (Ma) | ³⁶Ar/⁴⁰Ar |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | 500 | 3 | 2 | 1 | 3 / 2 / 1 | 26.86 | 0.333 |
| 2 | 550 | 9 | 6 | 0 | 12 / 8 / 1 | 26.86 | 0.000 |
| 3 | 600 | 0 | 14 | 0 | 12 / 22 / 1 | 0.00 | — |
| 4 | 650 | 0 | 15 | 0 | 12 / 37 / 1 | 0.00 | — |
| 5 | 700 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 12 / 37 / 1 | — | — |

The apparent-age transform uses an explicitly synthetic `J = 0.01` and the
total ⁴⁰K decay constant. It is a visualization of stepwise ⁴⁰Ar/³⁹Ar release,
not an age calibration. The first two old apparent-age steps followed by
trap-dominated ³⁹Ar release are the predicted direction of the 1998 recoil-trap
interpretation. The only ³⁶Ar leaves in the first step, so contamination is
front-loaded rather than tracking radiogenic Ar. The small demonstration does
not reproduce or fit the hydrothermally treated sample's full staircase; it
establishes the mechanism-to-spectrum path needed for that quantitative test.

## Sensitivity honesty

`docs/program/results/E2-muscovite-full-mechanism/sensitivity.csv` records the
pure Eyring rate multipliers for all three uncomputed phase-2 proxies:
extended-zone hopping at 40 kcal/mol, delamination at 58 kcal/mol, and
³⁹Ar trap escape at 64 kcal/mol, each bracketed by ±5 kcal/mol. Across
500–700 °C, the −5 kcal bracket is 25.90× to 13.27× nominal and the
+5 kcal bracket is 0.0386× to 0.0754× nominal. These are **rate-factor bands**,
not simulated confidence intervals and not computed barrier uncertainties.
They demonstrate that quantitative timing claims must wait for phase-3 NEB.
