# Pitting Corrosion of Passive Metals as Defect-Resolved Lattice KMC — Scoping Document

*Prepared 2026-08-18 for the petra/quarry research showcase program. Companion to petra/docs/DESIGN.md; the model sketch below uses the deck formalism as implemented (kinds/states/guards/`by_count` modifiers/`[thermo.mu]`/`[[defects]]` strain coupling).*

---

## 1. The problem and stakes

Pitting corrosion is the dominant failure mode of the passive metals civilization actually runs on — stainless steels, aluminum alloys, nickel alloys, titanium. A metal that "shouldn't" corrode (its passive oxide film dissolves at nm/year) fails catastrophically at a single point where the film breaks down and doesn't heal, drilling a pit that becomes a stress-crack initiator. NACE/AMPP's IMPACT study put the global cost of corrosion at ~US$2.5 trillion/year (~3.4% of GDP), and localized corrosion is the *unpredictable* fraction of that: general corrosion allowances can be engineered around; pitting is stochastic by nature. The canonical experimental facts are statistical: pitting potentials measured on nominally identical specimens scatter over 100+ mV and follow a normal-ish distribution; pit induction times are exponentially/Weibull distributed; maximum pit depths follow extreme-value (Gumbel) statistics — the classic engineering tool since Aziz's 1956 aluminum work ([AMPP Corrosion 12, 35](https://content.ampp.org/corrosion/article-abstract/12/10/35/26685/Application-of-the-Statistical-Theory-of-Extreme)).

The scientific claim of this showcase: **pitting is mathematically isomorphic to defect-driven mineral dissolution**, which is exactly what petra was built for. Both are (i) localized attack nucleating preferentially at crystallographic/microstructural defects, (ii) a competition between a protective-surface repair process (repassivation / hydroxylation) and a breakdown process (Cl⁻ attack / hydrolysis), (iii) stochastic initiation with heavy-tailed waiting-time statistics that only ensemble simulation can address, and (iv) morphology evolution (etch pits ↔ corrosion pits) driven by environment-dependent barriers. Petra's etch-pit demo — a screw dislocation whose strain field lowers detachment barriers so a pit drills down the dislocation line (`examples/kossel-etchpit.toml`) — *is already a pitting simulation* in every mathematical respect except vocabulary. The showcase is the translation: replace kaolinite chemistry with a passive-film state vocabulary, replace Δμ with electrode potential, and reproduce corrosion's signature statistics with an exact-in-time algorithm the corrosion-modeling community largely does not use.

The strategic stake for an independent scientist: corrosion modeling is a large, funded, application-hungry community (nuclear waste canisters, desalination, offshore, aerospace) whose stochastic-simulation tooling is, by the standards of the KMC literature, unsophisticated. A well-executed, open, reproducible ensemble-KMC study with matched statistics is a visible contribution achievable on one workstation.

## 2. Modeling literature map

**(a) Classical stochastic/mechanistic models.**
- **Shibata's stochastic theory** (1976–1996; [Corrosion 33, 243](https://content.ampp.org/corrosion/article-abstract/33/7/243/3898/Stochastic-Theory-of-Pitting-Corrosion)) treats pit generation as a birth–death process; it *explains* the observed distributions of pitting potential and induction time phenomenologically (rate parameters λ, μ fitted to data) but contains no spatial or mechanistic content. Still the reference frame for all pitting statistics.
- **Macdonald's Point Defect Model (PDM)** (1981–present; [PDM review](https://www.researchgate.net/profile/Digby-Macdonald-2/publication/235934425_The_Point_Defect_Model_for_the_Passive_State/links/550345100cf2d60c0e64e038/The-Point-Defect-Model-for-the-Passive-State.pdf); [chemical breakdown & pit initiation, JES 1981](https://iopscience.iop.org/article/10.1149/1.2127592)): passive film = defective oxide sustained by cation/oxygen vacancy fluxes; Cl⁻ absorbs into oxygen vacancies, autocatalytically generates cation-vacancy condensates at the metal/film interface, film locally collapses. Crucially, the PDM *derives* the observed near-normal distribution of breakdown potentials from an assumed distribution of vacancy diffusivities — distribution-matching is native to this school. Active extensions continue: [Bösing et al.'s PDM extension for chloride electrolytes (Corrosion Science 2025)](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0010938X25004937). But the PDM is mean-field and zero-dimensional per site: no lattice, no spatial coupling, no morphology.
- **Pit-stability criterion school (Frankel, Li, Scully)**: the "Passive Film Breakdown vs Pit Growth Stability" series Parts I–VI ([Part II, CPT model](https://iopscience.iop.org/article/10.1149/2.0591809jes); [Part VI, pitting/repassivation potentials](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0010938X21000433)) unifies pitting potential, repassivation potential, and critical pitting temperature via the Galvele pit-stability product and salt-film criteria. Deterministic continuum reasoning; the current mainstream conceptual framework. Any KMC showcase must speak this language (report the pit-stability product of simulated pits).

**(b) Cellular-automata corrosion.** A substantial, mostly 2010s–2020s literature: reviews in [Materials 2023](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10488826/) and a dedicated [2024 review of CA pitting simulation](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/379205269_Cellular_automata_simulation_of_pitting_corrosion_of_metals_A_review); representative recent work includes [3D CA of Q345 pits under salt spray (Corrosion Science 2019)](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0010938X18319905), [CA of pitting in marine environments (Materials Today Comm. 2024)](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S2352492824025364), [CA–FEM coupling for stress-assisted pitting (Materials 2025/2026)](https://doi.org/10.3390/ma19051001), [CA of metastable pit interactions](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/271590101_Cellular_automata_simulation_of_interactions_between_metastable_corrosion_pits_on_stainless_steel), and [CA of carbon steel salt-spray corrosion (npj Materials Degradation 2024)](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41529-024-00447-9). Update rules are synchronous, probabilistic, ad hoc: cells flip with tuned probabilities per neighborhood pattern; "time" is generations, mapped to hours after the fact by fitting. Sophisticated versions add diffusing acidity species and FEM stress feedback. Groups: mostly Chinese materials/nuclear institutes, plus the older French school (Taleb, Stafiej, di Caprio — CA with electrochemical local rules). There is also a discrete 3D stochastic pitting model tradition ([Reis, Stafiej et al.](https://www2.ifsc.usp.br/portal-ifsc/site-antigo/images/stories/PDF/06.pdf)).
- **What CA lacks**: physical time (no rates, no Arrhenius/Eyring provenance), exactness (synchronous updates create update-order artifacts), and calibrated statistics (ensembles are rarely run; when run, distributions are not quantitatively matched to experimental Weibull/Gumbel parameters).

**(c) KMC corrosion — the direct competition.** Sparse. [Simple KMC models of dissolution pitting at crystal defects (J. Chem. Phys. 2008, Sandia)](https://pubs.aip.org/aip/jcp/article/129/20/204106/907679/Simple-kinetic-Monte-Carlo-models-for-dissolution) is the closest ancestor — literally the etch-pit-at-dislocation problem, mineral-dissolution motivated, hard-coded. Atomistic MC of dealloying (Erlebacher's Au-Ag work; [Pt-catalyst dealloying KMC](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0013468613000856); [Policastro's chapter on atomistic MC of dissolution](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1002/9781119057666.ch4)) is mature but targets nanoporosity evolution, not passive-film breakdown statistics. A [Monte-Carlo + deterministic repassivation model (JES 2020)](https://iopscience.iop.org/article/10.1149/1945-7111/ab67a0) does pit populations but not lattice-resolved. There is no published work I can find doing **continuous-time, rejection-free lattice KMC of passive-film breakdown with ensemble statistics matched against measured pitting distributions**. That is the open lane.

**(d) Phase-field corrosion — the current fashion.** Explosive growth 2018→present: the foundational [npj Comput. Mater. 2018 pitting kinetics model](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41524-018-0089-4); Martínez-Pañeda's group (Oxford) as the most visible force — [MRS Bulletin 2024 overview](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1557/s43577-024-00715-8), [microstructure-sensitive electro-chemo-mechanical pitting + SCC model (2024)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2403.20301), plus MIC, EDL-kinetics, and concrete-rebar variants (2024–2026 arXiv). Phase field owns the *propagation* problem (moving interface, mechanics coupling, FEM ecosystems). It is deterministic and mesoscale: **initiation statistics are outside its ontology** — a phase-field pit starts because the modeler seeds it. This is a complement, not a competitor: KMC owns nucleation statistics; phase field owns late-stage growth.

**(e) Machine learning.** Mostly regression on inspection datasets (pipeline pit depths, [ML pitting-resistance prediction from alloy composition](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0927775723013584); [2025 in-situ monitoring review](https://www.degruyterbrill.com/document/doi/10.1515/corrrev-2025-0014/html)). No mechanistic content; irrelevant as competition, useful as a source of digitized pit-depth datasets.

**(f) The critical-phenomenon result to aim at**: [Punckt, Bölscher, Rotermund, Mikhailov, Organ, Budiansky, Scully, Hudson, *Science* 305, 1133 (2004)](https://science.sciencemag.org/content/305/5687/1133) — pitting onset is a cooperative critical phenomenon: metastable pits interact (local Cl⁻/acidity release weakens neighbors' films), producing explosive autocatalytic growth in pit number and spatial clustering near the pitting potential. This is a *spatially-coupled stochastic* phenomenon — precisely the regime where exact KMC beats both mean-field stochastic theory and deterministic phase field. Reproducing Punckt's clustering transition with matched statistics is the showcase's headline target.

**Active venues/groups 2020–2026**: Martínez-Pañeda (Oxford, phase field); Frankel/Fontana Center (OSU, mechanism + stability criteria); Scully/Hudson lineage (UVA); Macdonald (Berkeley, PDM); Chinese CA groups (USTB, Beihang, nuclear institutes); journals: *Corrosion Science*, *npj Materials Degradation*, *Electrochimica Acta*, *J. Electrochem. Soc.*, *Corrosion*.

## 3. The gap petra fills

1. **Exact continuous-time dynamics vs. CA generations.** Petra's rejection-free n-fold-way with Fenwick-tree selection gives physically-timed trajectories (Δt = −ln u/R). Every CA paper's "time calibration" step disappears; simulated induction-time distributions are in seconds, comparable to potentiostatic hold experiments *without* post-hoc scaling. Simulated current is literally the event-rate time series × charge per event — metastable pit transients fall out for free from the JSONL event log.
2. **Barriers with provenance.** CA rules are tuned probabilities. Petra rates are Arrhenius/Eyring with declared Ea, prefactor, and environment modifiers (`by_count` tables) — auditable in the deck, and later upgradeable by quarry: DFT cluster calculations of Cl⁻ insertion into O vacancies on Cr₂O₃/Fe₂O₃ exist in the literature ([Cl-induced breakdown of Fe₂O₃/Cr₂O₃, DFT](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S1005030222004327); [Cl in O-vacancies on α-Fe₂O₃](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0010938X18319978); [OSTI: Cl-induced degradation of α-Cr₂O₃](https://www.osti.gov/pages/biblio/2371688)) and are exactly quarry-sized (~1 hr/barrier on the 4090). Nobody in the CA literature has a first-principles path to their update rules; petra+quarry does, and its provenance story (deck cites the barrier's origin) is the same one built for kaolinite.
3. **Defects and strain natively.** Pits nucleate at MnS inclusions, grain boundaries, and dislocations. Petra's `[[init]]` probability/site rules seed defect populations reproducibly; `[[defects]]` strain fields already produce etch pits at dislocations. A dilute random "weak-site" population (the lattice analog of PDM's vacancy-diffusivity distribution or of inclusion density) is a two-line deck stanza — and the *distribution of weak-site strengths* becomes an explicit, fittable model input rather than a hidden assumption.
4. **Ensemble statistics as a first-class product.** The seed-sweep ensemble runner + f64 determinism means induction-time distributions, pitting-potential distributions (via potential-ramp protocol), and pit-depth extreme-value statistics come with error bars. The experimental targets are Weibull/Gumbel; the comparison is distribution-to-distribution (K-S tests, Weibull-parameter CIs), which almost no simulation paper in this field does.
5. **Electrochemical potential = the Δμ knob.** Attachment/detachment rates already carry `a·exp(Δμ/RT)`. In corrosion, anodic dissolution rates go as exp(αFη/RT) (Butler–Volmer): the applied potential enters exactly like a chemical-potential shift on electron-transfer events, i.e., `[thermo.mu]` reinterpreted as αFE. A potentiodynamic sweep is a time-dependent μ — a modest engine extension (scheduled thermo updates) that is also on the mineral roadmap (varying saturation state).
6. **The swappable-update-rule generalization has a story here too**: run the *same deck* under exact KMC and under a synchronous-CA update strategy and quantify the artifacts CA introduces in the very statistics the field cares about. That's a methods paper on its own.

## 4. Minimal viable model sketch (deck-ready)

**Geometry.** 2D-lattice-plus-depth abstraction, exactly like the kaolinite slab: a `[na, nb, nz]` lattice, `["periodic", "periodic", "open"]`, where the top open surface is the film/electrolyte interface and `fixed` bottom anchors bulk metal. v0 can even be single-layer (film state per surface column, depth as a counter via configs); v1 uses a true 3D slab so pit morphology is real. Simple cubic cell (Kossel-like), one site kind `M` (metal) initially; a second kind or occupant `Film` for the oxide layer above it.

**Species.** `Fe` (lumped metal cation), `vacantO`-style film states folded into configs; Cl⁻ enters via activity, not as explicit lattice occupant in v0.

**States (site kind `surf`, one column of the film; v0 vocabulary):**
- `intact` — passive film present, healthy
- `adsorbed` — Cl⁻ adsorbed / incorporated at film site (the PDM "Cl in O-vacancy" state)
- `thinned` — film locally degraded (cation-vacancy condensate forming)
- `bare` — film locally absent, metal exposed
- `dissolving` — active anodic dissolution (metal atoms detaching; in 3D slab this is `bare` metal sites detaching Kossel-style)
- `repassivated` — healed film (can simply return to `intact`; keep as separate state if hysteresis matters)
- plus (3D) metal sites: `occupied`/`empty` as in kossel.toml.

**Transitions (all `[[reactions]]` with Arrhenius rates; the potential knob E enters as a Δμ-like additive on electron-transfer steps):**
1. `Cl_adsorption`: `intact → adsorbed`, rate ∝ a_Cl · exp(αFE/RT). *This is the chloride-activity × potential handle.*
2. `Cl_desorption`: `adsorbed → intact` (reverse, detailed-balance linted).
3. `film_thinning`: `adsorbed → thinned`, with `by_count` modifier: each neighboring `thinned|bare|dissolving` site lowers Ea (autocatalysis — local acidification/Cl accumulation, the Punckt coupling). This modifier table is the model's heart.
4. `film_rupture`: `thinned → bare`, potential-accelerated; **strain-coupled** via `[[defects]]` (dislocations/inclusion stress lower the barrier → deterministic weak points) and/or an `[[init]]` `probability = p` pass seeding a fraction of sites in a `weak` config with lower rupture barrier (stochastic weak points — inclusion analog).
5. `metal_dissolution`: `bare` metal detaches (Kossel bond-counting `per_match`/`by_count` barrier — undercutting gives pit shape), rate ∝ exp(αFE/RT); each dissolution event *increments local aggressiveness*: implemented as guards on neighbors (a dissolving neighborhood keeps repassivation suppressed) — v0 proxy for pit acidification without explicit solution chemistry.
6. `repassivation`: `bare → intact`, rate *decreasing* with count of active neighbors (`by_count` with positive ΔEa per active neighbor) and decreasing with potential is optional; this is the breakdown-vs-repair competition. Metastable pit = a cluster that nucleates, grows a few dozen events, then loses the race and repassivates.

**Observables (all from the JSONL event log + snapshots):**
- **Induction time** per run = first passage to a pit surviving > N events or > depth d; ensemble over ≥10³ seeds → Weibull fit. λ(E, a_Cl, T) survival curves.
- **Pitting potential distribution**: potential-ramp protocol; E at first stable pit per seed → distribution vs. Shibata/experiment.
- **Current transient**: sum over events of (charge × rate) in time bins → metastable transient shapes (rise + sharp repassivation drop), compare to [304 SS transient data](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0010938X14002042).
- **Pit density and spatial statistics**: pair-correlation of pit sites vs. time → the Punckt clustering transition.
- **Pit morphology/depth distribution**: extreme-value (Gumbel) fit of max depth vs. time, vs. Aziz aluminum data; aspect ratios (open vs. occluded) as function of the undercut barrier table.
- **CPT analog**: temperature ramp at fixed E; the Arrhenius structure gives a genuine, non-inserted temperature dependence — a sharp stable-pitting onset temperature would be a strong result ([Frankel CPT model as comparison](https://iopscience.iop.org/article/10.1149/2.0591809jes)).

**Explicitly out of v0** (state honestly in the paper): solution-phase transport/ohmic drop inside pits, salt films, true Butler–Volmer kinetics beyond the transfer-coefficient approximation, film growth kinetics (high-field model), alloy composition. Each is a documented refinement, and several (depleting reservoir, scheduled thermo) are already on petra's roadmap.

## 5. Validation datasets (all published, all accessible)

1. **Metastable pitting transients, 304/316 SS in NaCl** — [Corrosion Science 2014, 304 SS in 3.5% NaCl](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0010938X14002042) (transient counts, lifetimes, peak currents vs. potential); Pistorius & Burstein's classic transition-to-stability datasets ([IAEA record](https://inis.iaea.org/records/fvnz3-ds516)); digitizable figures.
2. **Pitting potential / induction-time statistics** — Shibata's own datasets (normal E_pit distributions, exponential induction times on 304); [stochastic-modeling compilations](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0010938X06002150) (Valor et al.: nonhomogeneous Poisson + Weibull for initiation).
3. **Extreme-value pit depths** — Aziz 1956 aluminum-in-water Gumbel data; [marine steel EVS data (Melchers)](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0266892008000295); [generic max-pit-depth methodology](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0010938X11001806).
4. **Spatial clustering / cooperative onset** — Punckt 2004 Science supplementary (pit counts, cluster statistics vs. potential) and Budiansky/Scully's microelectrode-array interaction papers.
5. **CPT** — ASTM G150-style CPT values for 316/duplex grades scattered through the literature ([e.g., Metrohm app note conditions](https://www.metrohm.com/en/applications/application-notes/autolab-applikationen-anautolab/an-cor-006.html); [duplex weld CPT distributions](https://www.researchgate.net/figure/ASTM-G150-critical-pitting-temperature-for-spot-welds-in-three-duplex-grades-The_fig5_286677410)).
6. **Barrier provenance (later)** — published DFT numbers for Cl⁻/O-vacancy energetics on Fe₂O₃/Cr₂O₃ (§3.2 refs) as priors before quarry runs.

## 6. Phased plan (one person + agent + workstation)

- **Phase 0 (1–2 weeks): translation deck.** Write `passive-metal.toml` v0 (states/transitions above) against current petra — no engine changes needed except possibly a scheduled-μ hook. Verify detailed-balance lints, `--paranoid` runs, watch metastable pits in graph-viz live mode. *Exit: metastable transients visible; repassivation/breakdown competition qualitatively right.*
- **Phase 1 (2–4 weeks): statistics engine run.** Ensemble sweeps (10³ seeds × grid of E, a_Cl, T) — cheap: lattices of 10⁴–10⁵ sites, minutes/run on the 7950X, embarrassingly parallel across 16 cores. Fit Weibull induction times, transient shape statistics, survival curves. Calibrate the ~6 free barriers against dataset (1)+(2) via coarse grid + agent-driven refinement. *Exit: matched induction-time and transient statistics for one alloy/one environment.*
- **Phase 2 (2–3 weeks): the Punckt experiment in silico.** Turn up the autocatalytic `by_count` coupling; measure pit-number explosion and spatial clustering vs. E; identify the critical regime. This is the headline figure. *Exit: clustering transition reproduced qualitatively; scaling analysis.*
- **Phase 3 (2–4 weeks): defects + morphology.** 3D slab runs; dislocation strain fields and seeded weak sites; pit depth Gumbel statistics vs. time; CPT ramp protocol. WASM/graph-viz interactive demo ("watch a pit nucleate at a dislocation") as the showcase artifact. *Exit: extreme-value exponents compared to data; interactive demo public.*
- **Phase 4 (parallel, ongoing): quarry provenance.** Cl⁻ adsorption/insertion barriers on small (CrO)ₓ/(FeO)ₓ hydroxylated clusters; compare with published DFT; substitute computed barriers for one or two fitted ones and report sensitivity. *Exit: at least one deck barrier with computed provenance.*
- **Phase 5: paper + preprint** ("Exact kinetic Monte Carlo of passive-film breakdown: pit-initiation statistics from environment-dependent barriers"), plus optionally the KMC-vs-CA update-rule methods comparison enabled by the swappable-update-strategy work.

Total: ~3–4 months part-time. GPU is only needed in Phase 4; everything else is CPU-parallel ensembles.

## 7. Risks and reviewer skepticism

- **"Your lattice isn't a passive film."** Real films are 1–3 nm amorphous/nanocrystalline duplex oxides. Answer honestly: the lattice is a *mesoscopic site model* in the Shibata/PDM tradition, with space added — sites are film patches (~nm²), not atoms. Frame as coarse-grained KMC, not atomistic. Do not oversell atomistic fidelity.
- **"No solution chemistry / ohmic drop / salt film — pit stability is transport-controlled."** True for *stable growth*; the showcase deliberately targets *initiation and metastable statistics*, where surface-state kinetics dominate. Cite Frankel Parts I–VI to show awareness; position phase field as the correct tool downstream and KMC upstream. The neighbor-count autocatalysis proxy must be labeled a proxy.
- **"Parameters are fitted, so what did we learn?"** Six-ish barriers vs. ~10+ independent measured statistics (transient shapes, distribution families and their parameter trends vs. E, a_Cl, T) — overdetermination is the defense. Report identifiability (which data constrain which barrier). The quarry-provenance phase converts this from weakness to roadmap.
- **"Physical time claims require real prefactors."** Prefactors of surface electrochemical steps are poorly known; be explicit that absolute times are calibrated via one datum (e.g., mean transient rate at one potential) and *distribution shapes/scalings* are the predictions.
- **"Isn't this just the 2008 Sandia KMC / dealloying MC again?"** Differentiators: declarative decks (reviewable physics), exact ensemble statistics matched to experimental distributions, film-breakdown state machine (not pure dissolution), defect/strain coupling, open + reproducible + in-browser. Cite both lineages generously.
- **Field-outsider risk.** A geochemist walking into Corrosion Science gets read carefully. Mitigations: use the field's own vocabulary (pit stability product, E_pit, E_rp, CPT), validate against their canonical datasets, ideally recruit one corrosion-community collaborator or at minimum solicit pre-submission feedback (Frankel and Macdonald both engage publicly). The mineral-dissolution-to-corrosion isomorphism has a respectable pedigree (Lasaga's etch-pit theory is cited in corrosion papers) — lead with it as a strength, not an apology.
- **Technical risk: stiffness.** Fast adsorb/desorb flip-flopping (reaction 1/2) may dominate steps — petra's stiffness detection will flag it; mitigation is lumping the adsorption pre-equilibrium into the thinning rate (standard trick, documented).
